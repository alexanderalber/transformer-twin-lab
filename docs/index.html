transformer-twin-lab

Interactive demos on uncertainty in transformer digital twins.

  • Duval Triangle 1, under measurement uncertainty

    One DGA result in Duval Triangle 1, and the cloud of diagnoses it could equally have meant. Push the relative measurement uncertainty from 5 to 35 percent and the point starts crossing zone boundaries.

  • Duval Pentagon 1 and 2, under measurement uncertainty

    The same question in five gases. One point in Duval Pentagon 1 and 2, and the region of diagnoses that the laboratory error bar really covers.

  • Thermal mini-twin, IEC 60076-7

    A year of top-oil and hot-spot temperature computed from a load profile, and four ways to propagate the uncertainty in it into the remaining life of the insulation.

  • Five levels of a digital twin

    One transformer, one thermal model, five jobs: describe the state, diagnose a cooling fault, forecast the next 48 hours, choose between dispatch options, and close the loop.

Sources: IEC 60599 and CIGRE TB 771 (Duval Triangle 1 fault zones); Duval and Lamarre 2014 and CIGRE TB 771 (Duval Pentagon 1 and 2); IEC 60076-7 (loading guide); IEC 60076-2 (temperature-rise limits); CIGRE TB 738 (aging under moisture); Lundgaard et al. 2004 (aging kinetics); Parton and Logan 1981 (diurnal temperature shape). Everything runs locally in your browser, nothing is uploaded.
